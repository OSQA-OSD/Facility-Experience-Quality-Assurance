        Cloud backup

        • Saved at once. Every report is written to the database the moment it is submitted or changed, and stays there for good. Nothing is ever thrown away: every change is a numbered version, a deleted report waits in the archive below, photos are never removed.
        • Backed up by itself — nothing to press. Every 10 minutes whatever changed (new versions, the audit and review history, new photos) is copied to separate cloud storage. Every night at 02:30 a copy of the accounts, buildings and assignments is added: the last 30 nights, and the first night of every month for good.
        • The database can also be put back to any minute of the last 30 days.
        Optional — keep a copy on this deviceTake the data out at any time. Two files that load straight back into a new database: the data (small), and the photos (can be large — best on a computer).
