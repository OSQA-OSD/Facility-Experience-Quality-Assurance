Features

Everything quality teams need, in one place

From the first assignment to the final report, every step happens in the same workspace.

Structured inspections

Ten sections from Arrival & Parking to Washrooms, scored item by item with comments and photos.

Assign & track

Officers assign buildings for each quarter and watch every auditor's completion rate update live.

Team overview

Leaders follow assignments, results and progress by division, area and building.

Reports & analytics

Filter every inspection, then break results down by division, area, building, quarter or inspector.

Notifications

Auditors hear about new assignments; officers and leaders hear the moment a building is completed.

Exports

A PDF for every inspection, CSV for the data and PNG for any chart.

Roles

Built for every role on the team

Accounts are issued by an administrator, and each person sees only what their role needs.

Quality AdminCreates accounts and sets each person's permissions.
Quality LeaderOversees officers, auditors and results across divisions.
Quality OfficerAssigns buildings and tracks auditor progress live.
Quality AuditorReceives assignments and completes the inspections.
Data AnalystBuilds filtered reports by division, area and building.
How it works

Three steps to a complete report

The same three steps you'll see at the top of every inspection.

1Details

Choose the building, date, inspection type (BOQI, EOQI or follow-up) and division.

2Sections

Score each of the ten sections, adding comments and photos wherever they help.

3Score Card

Review the overall score and each section, save the inspection and export the PDF.
